About Me

My creative journey began at 7, learning Photoshop through simple masking and photo edits that grew into a real passion for graphic design, and at 12 I started video editing with Filmora and CapCut before moving to Premiere Pro and After Effects, where my design background made motion graphics come naturally.

On August 20, 2023 I launched my YouTube channel with Minecraft videos and hit 1K subscribers within a year, then started designing thumbnails for other creators, explored 3D in Cinema 4D and Blender, and spent 2024 to 2025 working as an editor in the Minecraft community.

Today I focus on gaming documentaries that tell the stories behind games and creators, my channel has grown to 7.5K subscribers, and I work as a video editor, thumbnail artist, motion designer and 3D animator, creating content that grabs attention and keeps viewers watching.
